\section*{How to use this book}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How to use this book}
This book merges two collections of hard Digital SAT mathematics
questions into one sequence, with duplicates removed and the problems
regrouped by the four tested domains.

Every page carries at most \textbf{three} problems. The space left under
each one is deliberate: these questions are not meant to be read, they
are meant to be worked, and the room to work is part of the page.

The answer key at the back lists every published answer. Where a source
did not publish one, the entry is marked \textemdash; solve those and
fill them in yourself.
